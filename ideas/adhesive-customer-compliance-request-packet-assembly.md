# Adhesive Customer Compliance Request Packet Assembly

## Problem statement

Adhesive manufacturers receive customer requests for VOC certificates, LEED declarations,
IMDS submissions, compliance letters, audit responses, questionnaires, and qualification
packages. A current Matrix Adhesives posting assigns one product-stewardship leader to build
a structured intake process and 10-day service target while also establishing a centralized
supplier-document repository and regulatory software; until that infrastructure exists, the
posting specifies SharePoint or Excel for the regulatory database. An official Henkel
Adhesive Technologies posting separately assigns product-safety staff to manage customer
requests in a query tool and follow up with contributors to obtain the inputs needed to close
them.

## Automation hypothesis

SPECULATIVE. A request-intake layer could classify each customer's requested document set,
retrieve approved formulation and supplier-declaration facts from the manufacturer's
regulatory repository, route missing fields to the responsible technical contributor, and
produce a review-ready response or portal submission with source and version traceability.
This depends on customer formats being sufficiently repeatable, on product and supplier data
being available in structured form, and on a qualified product-stewardship reviewer retaining
final responsibility for representations made to customers.
